Complex 6

About This Project

Complex 6 is a masterclass in futuristic, high-density design, catering to the ultimate tech-driven lifestyle. The building features an automated subterranean parking system and a fleet of shared electric vehicles available exclusively for residents via a proprietary mobile app.

The living units are "smart-first," equipped with voice-activated lighting, climate control, and glass partitions that can switch from transparent to opaque at the touch of a button. Designed for the modern professional, each apartment includes a built-in, soundproofed workspace, making it the premier choice for those at the cutting edge of the digital economy.
