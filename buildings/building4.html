Complex 4

About This Project

Complex 4 is a standout example of industrial-chic living, featuring raw concrete accents and expansive, open-air walkways. The central hub is a high-tech "maker space" designed for creative professionals, complemented by a communal rooftop garden that offers panoramic views of the industrial district.

The units inside prioritize a minimalist, modular layout that allows residents to easily customize their living space. With polished concrete floors and exposed ductwork, the aesthetic is intentionally bold, providing a unique, modern backdrop for those seeking a lifestyle that is both functional and edgy.
