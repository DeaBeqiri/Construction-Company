Complex 5

About This Project

Complex 5 is defined by its commitment to sustainable, eco-conscious living. The architecture seamlessly integrates vertical gardens and solar-paneled roofing, while the ground floor features a curated organic market and a zero-waste café for residents.

The interiors prioritize wellness, using non-toxic materials and advanced air-filtration systems to create a healthy sanctuary. Large cedar-framed windows overlook a central bio-pond, offering a tranquil, nature-focused atmosphere right in the middle of the city.
